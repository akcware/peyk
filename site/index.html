Sixty mails a day, and every one of them shouts.

A newsletter, an invoice, a landlord, a school nurse: the inbox gives them all the same red badge. So you check constantly, or you miss the one that counts.

Proactive Agent reads all sixty, rates each one, and interrupts you for the few that need you today. The rest waits for tomorrow's morning brief.

One queue. One judgment. One gate.

Importance and interruption are different questions. A model answers the first; a plain function with a daily budget answers the second.

1

Observe

Every mail, calendar event and chat message becomes an observation in one queue. Nothing is handled twice; nothing is dropped.

2

Triage

The model rates urgency 1 to 5 and writes a one-line, secretary-style summary in your language.

3

Budget gate

Deterministic: daily quota (default 5), per-thread cooldown, quiet hours, mutes. One slot stays reserved for urgency-5 emergencies.

4

Notify

A ping in Telegram with 👍 useful, 👎 noise and 🔇 mute thread. Your taps tune what gets through.

5

Act

Drafts, reminders and a morning brief at 08:00 in your timezone. Every outbound action waits for your approval.

Chat like a secretary
First a reflex, "Sure, checking today's mail…", then the answer with facts from real observations. It remembers what it told you, so "that mail" works. It finds contacts itself and keeps the durable facts you tell it. "Remind me tomorrow 9:00: invoice" just works.
Measured
49 seconds from a mail being sent to the Telegram ping, bounded by Composio's polling. Triage eval: 59 of 60 mails within one urgency point, 100% recall on emergencies.
Adding a source
Is a data change. Google Calendar arrived as one mapping row with zero changes to the core, and a test keeps it that way.

Approval, not autonomy.

Every outbound action is a draft you see first. An edit changes the draft's content hash, and a Send button only works for the hash it was made for. An old button can never send a draft you changed.

Set up in one conversation.

  1. 1

    Say hi

    Message the bot. It introduces itself and asks who you are.

    hi
    Hi, I'm your proactive agent. I watch your mail and calendar and only interrupt when it matters. Who am I talking to?
  2. 2

    Answer one question

    Your name and where you are. That sets your timezone, your budget and your own memory.

    Alex, in Berlin
    Nice to meet you, Alex. Berlin time it is. Want me to connect your Gmail and Google Calendar?
  3. 3

    Tap the link

    It sends a Google sign-in link and waits until the account is connected. Nothing is typed into the chat.

    yes
    Here's your Gmail login: connect.composio.dev/… I'll tell you when it's linked.
    Gmail connected. I'm watching now.

No commands. No passwords in chat. Anyone can message the bot; each person gets their own budget, memory and timezone.
